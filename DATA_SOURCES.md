# Data sources and attribution

These dashboards distribute public aggregate data and derived screening results, with attribution and limitations retained in their methodology panels. They do not contain nonpublic company pipeline or contact data.

- **U.S. Census Bureau:** 2025 county cartographic boundaries/internal points; 2024 ACS five-year B25032 renter-occupied units in structures with five or more units. Census public-use/citation statement: https://www.census.gov/about/policies/citation.html . Source tables: https://data.census.gov/table/ACSDT5Y2024.B25032 . Federal agency statistical data are public-use data; these derived conclusions are ShineIQ's responsibility.
- **U.S. Energy Information Administration:** 2025 EIA-861 sector revenue/sales benchmarks and EIA Electric Power Monthly matched January–August 2025/2026 state averages. https://www.eia.gov/electricity/data/eia861/ and https://www.eia.gov/electricity/monthly/ . Source attribution is retained.
- **HUD:** Public LIHTC historical project database, 1987–2024, revised May 28, 2026. https://www.huduser.gov/portal/datasets/lihtc/property.html . Only public county-level counts and public unresolved-source records used by the dashboard are included. Distinct HUD IDs may describe phases or repeat awards, rather than unique buildings.
- **European Commission, Joint Research Centre, PVGIS 5.3:** Derived 1 kW DC photovoltaic performance results, ERA5 weather 2005–2023; crystalline silicon, fixed 20° south-facing, ventilated mounting, 14% system losses, horizon disabled. https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/photovoltaic-geographical-information-system-pvgis/using-pvgis-5_en . © European Union; source attribution retained. ERA5 source: Copernicus Climate Change Service / ECMWF. These are derived modeled results, not redistribution of the underlying weather archive.
- **HIFLD:** Archived public electric retail service territory context used in documented utility-reference assignments. No proprietary utility-territory product is included.

**Software:** V1 bundles D3 v7.9.0, copyright 2010–2023 Mike Bostock, under the ISC license. The complete permission notice is retained at v1/vendor/LICENSE-D3.txt.

Census/geometry, EIA, HUD, PVGIS-derived results and the source/method annotations are the same data used by the original public-data dashboards. No government agency or European institution endorses these screening conclusions.
