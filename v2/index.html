AUDIT TRAIL

What the numbers mean—and what they don't.

01

Solar productivity

kWh AC per kW DC annually. PVGIS 5.3 with ERA5 historical weather (2005–2023), crystalline silicon, 20° fixed tilt, south-facing, ventilated mounting, 14% system-loss input. One Census county internal reference point per county. Same setup everywhere. The 595 W estimate is yield multiplied by 0.595.

PVGIS official documentation ↗
02

Electricity price

2025 EIA annual realized average $/kWh. Utility-specific benchmark where a defensible territory assignment is available, or an explicitly labeled state average. Includes fixed-charge revenue. Not a marginal avoided-cost tariff or solar net-metering rate. Residential and commercial sectors stay separate.

EIA-861 electricity data ↗
03

Combined value

Modeled annual kWh/kWdc × EIA average retail $/kWh = annual dollars/kWdc. A gross retail-value screening index, not a building's actual achievable savings or a return-on-investment calculation.

Download original county study ↗
04

High on both

Both rate and solar yield at or above the national two-thirds quantile (equal weight per county). Thresholds remain fixed under geographic filtering. A high combined value does not guarantee passing this two-factor test.

05

Multifamily inventory

2024 ACS five-year B25032 counts renter-occupied homes in 5+ unit structures, not the number of apartment buildings. HUD LIHTC records are historical properties placed in service, not current sales prospects. These layers are populated only when the official inventory source files are bundled by the build or explicitly imported. A missing dataset remains unknown, never zero. Distinct HUD IDs may represent separate phases or repeat awards at the same site.

ACS B25032 ↗HUD LIHTC database ↗
06

Multifamily tariffs / ETB

Tenant meters may take residential rates; common/master meters can use either sector. Real bill savings requires actual schedules, self-consumption, export tariffs, eligibility, and ownership allocation. No ETB tariff rates are claimed to be imported until the harvest ZIP is supplied.

07

Solar geographic refinement

V2 includes an executable multi-point PVGIS validation script and importer. Until that rerun is performed, the base map still uses the original single county reference point; a neighboring-county difference is not a substitute for within-county simulation.

08

2026 price comparison

The rate-shift layer compares EIA state residential or commercial averages for January-August 2026 versus January-August 2025 (Electric Power Monthly Table 5.6.B; released October 23, 2026). 2026 is preliminary. Each county inherits its state result; this is not a utility-specific tariff change and it does not replace the original 2025 annual benchmark. Optional ETB tariff snapshots remain separate.
